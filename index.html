Our Mission

Rooted in purpose,
built for people

Saltus Ventures is a development company focused on creating responsible, well-designed communities. Founded by the team behind Knighton Architecture + Planning, we bring decades of design expertise to the development side of the equation.

Every project begins with a deep respect for the land and the people who will live there. We believe that thoughtful development has the power to shape stronger neighborhoods and a more sustainable future.

Based in
Provo, Utah
What Guides Us

Development done differently

Community-centered

Every decision is measured against how it impacts the people and neighborhoods around it. We develop for the long term, not the quick return.

Responsible growth

We respect the land and the context of each site. Responsible development means building what belongs — not just what fits.

Design-driven

As an extension of an architecture firm, design quality is in our DNA. We bring precision, craft, and intentionality to every project.
